Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ORBY ("we", "us") is a career practice app made by Joseph Ananth, an individual developer based in India. This policy explains what ORBY collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: josephananth7@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email address and an account ID. With Apple you can choose to hide your email.
  • Profile you give us: your name, job role, five-year goal, strengths and the areas you want to improve.
  • Progress: XP, SP, streak, moons and planets, suits, the plan we build for you, and your mission results (which skill, which game, your score). We store these so you can continue on a new phone and so your weekly plan adapts.
  • Your answers: what you type or say in missions and voice sessions is sent to our AI provider to score it and write feedback. We don't keep the text of your answers on our servers.
  • Voice: when you answer out loud, your iPhone's speech recogniser (provided by Apple) shows a live caption, and a speech model on your phone writes the final text. We receive only the text; your recording is never uploaded to us or stored by us.
  • Feedback you send: if you use Send feedback, we store your note, its type and an optional rating.
  • App usage and crashes: we may record which features are used (for example, a mission started or finished) and crash reports, linked to your account ID (never your name or email), so we can fix problems and improve the app.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. We do not show ads and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

Only to run and improve ORBY: sign you in, save and restore your progress, build your daily missions and weekly plan, coach your answers, and fix bugs. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores your account, profile, progress and feedback.
  • Google (Gemini API) generates missions, plans and feedback from your profile and answers.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases and the live caption while you speak; Google provides Google Sign-In. Apple's recogniser may send the audio to Apple to make the live caption, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and PostHog (usage analytics, EU hosting), when enabled.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of which plan you have (free week, monthly or one-time).

Each processes data under its own terms and only to provide its service to ORBY. Some of these providers store data outside India.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Mission results older than 90 days are dropped automatically. Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account, profile and progress from our servers and your phone. You can also email us.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll send you a copy or fix it.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Microphone: you can refuse access; every voice session can be typed instead.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can access, correct and erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. If you live in the EU or UK you have similar rights under the GDPR. We reply within 30 days.

Grievance officer: Joseph Ananth · josephananth7@gmail.com

Children

ORBY is for working professionals and isn't directed at children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has used ORBY, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each signed-in user read and change their own records.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
