Support

Something not working, or an idea for ORBY? Email josephananth7@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. You can also use Profile → Send feedback in the app.

Common questions

How do I get my progress on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before. Your XP, streak, planets and plan come back automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, profile and progress from our servers and your phone. You can also email us to ask.

The microphone isn't working in voice sessions.

Check iOS Settings → ORBY → Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can always type your answers instead.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → ORBY, and the reminder time in Profile → Daily reminder.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us from the address on your account and we'll send it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
